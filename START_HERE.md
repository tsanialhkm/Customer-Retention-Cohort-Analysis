# Investigation Brief

## Your role

Business Analyst, Alder & Finch Retail

## Business situation

The CRM dashboard shows that the repeat purchase rate for newly acquired customers fell from **72.55% in July 2011** to **28.80% in November 2011**.

Marketing believes customer quality has deteriorated and requests **GBP 30,000** for an emergency voucher campaign. Finance will not approve the proposal until the decline is validated.

## Objective

Determine whether new-customer retention genuinely deteriorated, quantify any material measurement problems, and recommend whether management should approve the campaign.

## Required final answer

Your case closure memo must answer:

1. Can you reproduce the dashboard figures?
2. What population, numerator, denominator, and reporting cutoff does the metric use?
3. Are the monthly cohorts directly comparable?
4. What does a fair retention comparison show?
5. How much of the order population can be linked to an identified customer?
6. Should management approve the proposed campaign?
7. What KPI definition and control should replace the current process?

## Rules

- Keep at least three plausible hypotheses open at the start.
- Do not assume that a mathematically correct dashboard is a valid decision metric.
- Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Cite query outputs or evidence files for every material claim.
- State what the available data cannot prove.
- Do not search for a public solution. This case was created for this project.
