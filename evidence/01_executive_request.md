# Request from the Managing Director

**From:** Maya Holt, Managing Director  
**To:** Business Analysis  
**Subject:** Validate the retention decline before campaign approval

Marketing reports that repeat purchase rate for new customers declined from 72.55% in July to 28.80% in November.

The team wants GBP 30,000 for an emergency voucher campaign. Before I approve it, determine whether retention genuinely deteriorated or whether the dashboard is creating a misleading comparison.

I need:

1. a reproducible analysis;
2. the size and cause of any measurement problem;
3. a decision on the campaign;
4. a retention KPI that management can use consistently.

Do not recommend spending based only on a downward-looking chart.
