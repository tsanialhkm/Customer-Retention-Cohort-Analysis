# KPI Dictionary, version 1.2

**Last updated:** 5 January 2011  
**Owner:** CRM Analytics

## New customer

An identified customer whose first qualifying order is observed during the reporting period.

## Repeat customer

A new customer who later places at least one additional qualifying order.

## Repeat purchase rate

```text
repeat customers / new customers
```

## Qualifying order

A completed sales invoice with positive quantity and positive unit price.

## Known gaps

The current definition does not specify:

- a fixed number of days in which the repeat order must occur;
- how long a cohort must be observed before publication;
- treatment of customers without a customer identifier;
- whether previously published cohort rates may change on later report runs.
